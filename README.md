# VentyH8 Fan Design

## Project Overview
The VentyH8 Fan Design project involves creating a **3D CAD model of an industrial fan** with complete housing, blades, and mounting structure. The design focuses on efficient airflow, structural integrity, and manufacturability. The fan design was developed for reliable performance in industrial environments while considering ease of assembly and maintenance.

---

## Project Goals
- Design fan blades and housing for optimized airflow
- Ensure structural stability of the fan assembly
- Develop a design suitable for industrial manufacturing
- Provide easy maintenance and assembly access

---

## Design Work
- 3D CAD modeling of fan blades, hub, and housing
- Structural frame and mounting design
- Dimensioning and tolerance setup
- Preparation of manufacturing-ready technical drawings

---

## Manufacturing Considerations
- Components designed for **metal fabrication and casting**
- Blade and housing profiles optimized for airflow efficiency
- Mounting structure designed for stability and vibration resistance
- Design allows easy assembly and maintenance

---

## Deliverables
- Complete 3D CAD assembly model
- Individual component models
- Technical drawings
- Manufacturing-ready design files

---

## Design Challenges
- Optimizing **blade geometry** for efficient airflow
- Ensuring **structural stability** at high rotational speeds
- Maintaining precise **tolerances** for assembly
- Designing housing to reduce vibration and noise

---

## Files Included
- CAD models (assembly and individual parts)
- Technical drawings
- Manufacturing documentation
